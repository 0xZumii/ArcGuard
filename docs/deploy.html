Read this before you click

  • This deploys to Arc mainnet (chain 5042). The gas is real USDC — roughly a cent.
  • The contract is ownerless: no admin, no upgrade path, no withdrawal. Once it is deployed, nobody (including you) can change it. That is deliberate.
  • Your private key never leaves your wallet. This page only asks the wallet to send one transaction.
  • After it deploys, copy the address into docs/config.js and commit it. That is the only wiring step.
