Three things that are true about Arc, and that scams rely on you not knowing

  • ARC is not publicly tradable. Circle genesis-minted 10 billion ARC and described it as a technical milestone, not a public launch: ARC is not used for gas, staking or governance, and no public sale has been announced. Any ARC you can actually buy is not Circle's ARC.
  • Real USDC bridging calls depositForBurn on the CCTP TokenMessenger contract (0x28b5…cf5d). It never asks you to send USDC to a wallet address.
  • Arc had no airdrop, no faucet on mainnet, and no rewards programme. Anywhere offering to claim, vote on, or unlock those things is inventing them.

What this tool does not do

  • It is not an oracle. It never says "safe". It reports what it observed and what it could not see.
  • It cannot catch a scam it has never seen a pattern of. It recognises the documented Arc patterns, not novel ones.
  • The community registry is uncurated public reports, not a verified blocklist. Reports can be wrong and can be filed by anyone — read them, including who filed them.
  • A clone of the authorised front end can still drain you by asking for a signature this page has never been taught to read.
  • Bytecode reading is heuristic. Decompiled intent is not intent.
